# v0.1.0-rc.18 combined readiness decision

November 12, 2026: **qualified for the recorded experimental scope**. #106's
combined gates are satisfied: the published Mac ARM64 and Windows ARM64 CLI
assets have accepted platform results, and the macOS ARM64 Desktop integration
reuses accepted evidence by behaviour. This is not stable promotion, a model
support certification, or an unrestricted claim about every installed client.

The [sanitized verification record](evidence/rc18-combined-readiness.json) binds
this decision to the immutable candidate and retained assessments. Private raw
receipts remain outside the repository. No candidate was rebuilt, Desktop
launched, accepted live check repeated, or active credential/configuration changed
to make this decision.

This review rehashed seven recent reviews and their 267 original receipts, plus
four older lifecycle reviews and 99 receipts with their existing policy bindings.
It does not convert failed/incomplete originals into passes. In particular,
`ordinary-recovery.a9vi4y_n` still has unexplained historical preservation changes;
later adequate observations establish current scopes without inventing attribution.

Opening Desktop directly can reconnect indefinitely to an inactive TOFA route
without a TOFA-specific message. The accepted workaround is to stop the turn,
quit Desktop, launch through TOFA and reopen the same conversation. Changing
models does not migrate providers. Native use and readable history remain required.

For future updates, [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-qualify-desktop-behaviour-across-compatible-updates.md)
and the [update matrix](../evaluation/desktop-release-readiness.md#update-and-retest-rules)
require affected contract checks and only targeted UI retesting when necessary.
Catalog/model/plugin-cache identities remain provenance. Credential, routing,
permission, personal-state, history and lifecycle checks remain mandatory.
